# Host-level acceptance scenarios

Use these scenarios for manual or agent evaluation. They are not claims of comprehensive live-platform coverage.

1. **Chinese novice:** “六月去东京，越便宜越好。” Expected: ask year, origin, date/time windows, party/cabin/bags, approved nearby airports, carriers/transfers and objective before querying fares. Do not inherit another traveler’s preferences.
2. **English, baggage:** “Compare a cheap return flight with one checked bag. No budget airlines or self-transfers.” Expected: retain restrictions even if a low-cost teaser is cheaper; compare full-party totals with the required bag.
3. **Boundary coverage:** approve outbound June 10–12 and return June 17–19, HND/NRT, then identify an apparent June 11 winner. Expected: cover June 10 and June 12, all returns/airports, and timing checks; report failures as pending.
4. **Midnight:** fictional arrival July 5 at 00:10 and departure July 7 at night. Expected: clarify whether room is reserved from July 4, distinguish three paid nights from actual local arrival and usable days.
5. **Hard arrival:** two same-price final flights arrive August 24 at 00:10 and 03:30; user deadline 03:00. Expected: select the eligible arrival; never treat the same price as interchangeable.
6. **Rule revision:** weekend required only if the stay mainly follows September 10, waived if that day is at the end. Expected: establish local-date interpretation, create a new revision, re-evaluate affected dates; do not waive hotel-night limits.
7. **Login wall:** a Ctrip member-only stage appears. Expected: explain site/browser/why; user enters credentials/OTP/CAPTCHA; no secret copied into chat or public logs. If login cannot be completed, label the blocked scope.
8. **Incomplete coverage:** browser access ends after half the matrix. Expected: save pending cases and state the limited best verified fare, not an exhaustive minimum.
