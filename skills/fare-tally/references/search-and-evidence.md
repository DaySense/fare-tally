# Search and verification

## Avoid date and route blind spots

- Give each approved route family an ID. A home-country stop before a holiday differs from one after it; distribute effort across all families.
- Enumerate airport alternatives for each leg. Label open jaws/ground legs and their unknown costs; airport changes are not free connections.
- Use explicit inclusive dates. ±2 days means five dates, including **earlier** ones. Outbound, return, intermediate legs and stays have independent domains.
- Do not infer hotel nights from departure-day gaps. Overnight/+2-day flights change actual arrival. Build broad enough candidate windows for accepted journey durations; verify stays from selected flights afterward.
- A new arrival pattern may expose an unsearched case inside agreed bounds. Add it; do not silently expand dates beyond them.
- Treat the script's matrix as a to-do list, never an automated guessed-URL grid. Teasers prioritize checks; they cannot eliminate unsearched package combinations.
- Audit both endpoints and the day before/after apparent winners within bounds, adjacent returns, all accepted stays and airport gaps. Date coverage is not timing/carrier coverage: check useful early/day/evening flights and fare/baggage bundles for finalists.
- Keep failed/blocked searches pending. A completed query grid still does not prove every airline, flight, fare bucket or sale channel was examined.

## Whole-booking evidence

Select the full multi-city/open-jaw sequence for the actual party/cabin/bags. Read updated UI state after every selection; do not mistake old cards during loading for the next stage. Final flight, carrier or airport changes require full repricing. Separately purchased tickets are a different, explicitly accepted scenario with different protection and bag rules.

At the final itinerary list, match the **correct** last flight and full arrival date/time. Same-price later arrivals may fail a hard deadline. Record displayed total, selected previous legs, source and timestamp. Distinguish final-list from pre-payment evidence. Do not create holds, enter identity, accept terms or pay for research alone.

Use supported tools and current visible controls. Close observed stale-search notices and retry an inconsistent state through a fresh query where appropriate. Do not repeatedly patch broken selections, use hidden endpoints or bypass CAPTCHA. Empty/loading pages are not confirmed no-results.

Sanitize links: remove session tokens, signed/account URLs and personal data. A city/date URL may not restore selected flights; supply identifiers and say so. Keep screenshots/ledgers in private research storage outside the skill repository.

## Practical eligibility

Check actual operating airports, not just metro codes; terminal/airport changes; codeshare duplication; total elapsed journey; ticket protection; check-in/immigration and baggage on self-transfers even without checked bags. One OTA sale does not itself prove protection.

For documents, distinguish the exact OTA badge from current official immigration/airline evidence for that connection and traveler. Unknown requirements remain unresolved. Free transit lodging counts only if current official terms establish eligibility for this ticket/passenger.

Separate flight-local timestamps, paid hotel reservation nights and usable days. A 00:10 arrival may need the previous night's room; a 01:00 departure may allow evening checkout. Use actual dates and date-aware timezones, including daylight-saving differences. Apply conditional weekend rules to local presence and agreed usable time.

## Changed preferences

Create a new revision with the changed rule, retain old files and re-evaluate affected cases, including newly admissible adjacent dates. Reuse saved fares only as historical unless rechecked. A relaxed weekend condition does not waive stay counts or arrival deadlines. Do not attribute quote fluctuations solely to date or timing.
